# Pitch assets (M6)

The demo script, expected judge questions with answers, and the 6-slide outline
live in [`../docs/06-M6-integration-and-pitch.md`](../docs/06-M6-integration-and-pitch.md).

Drop the built artefacts here:

- `slides.pdf` — export a PDF, do not rely on the venue having your fonts
- `demo-notes.md` — your own cue card, whatever form helps you
- `screenshots/` — fallback stills in case the laptop or projector fails

Two things to do before you leave for the venue:

1. Rehearse against `samples/01`, `02`, `04` in that order, three times, timed.
2. Run the whole demo with `GEOIP_PROVIDER=mock` and `DEMO_MODE=true` so you know
   it works with no network.
